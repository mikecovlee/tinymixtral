## 3. Distillation & share-alike caveats

- **Model-output content.** Several SFT sources (OpenHermes, SlimOrca, OpenOrca,
  MetaMath, ORCA-Math, OpenMathInstruct-2) consist largely of outputs generated by
  upstream LLMs (GPT-3.5/GPT-4/Llama-family). Their cards license the *annotation
  layer*, not the generated text's provenance; provider terms for the raw generations
  are upstream of this project.
- **Prompt lineage.** OpenOrca/SlimOrca prompts derive from ShareGPT and UltraChat
  user conversations (community Terms-of-Use exposure; redistribution risk lies with
  the originating dataset, not TinyMixtral).
- **Share-alike.** SQuAD v2 (CC-BY-SA-4.0) and Wikipedia (CC-BY-SA-3.0/GFDL) appear
  in small proportions inside our blends. The prevailing industry reading is that
  training on, and shipping weights of, a small share-alike corpus requires
  attribution rather than relicensing the model; we follow that and additionally link
  every source above.
- **ODC-BY attribution.** FineWeb-Edu and C4 require attribution to their producers;
  this document plus the links above constitute that attribution.
